Introduction

Our company builds software for modern businesses. We provide cloud
applications, APIs, and data platforms.

Technology

Our backend services use Python and FastAPI. We use PostgreSQL for
relational data and Redis for caching and conversation memory.

Architecture

The RAG system extracts documents, creates chunks, generates local
embeddings, and stores vectors in Weaviate Cloud.

Interview Process

Candidates can ask questions about the company and request an interview.
The assistant collects the candidate's name, email, date, and preferred
time before creating a booking.
